I built this site to keep up with AI Safety research and news. Every day an automated job collects new posts from the sources below and new arXiv papers, and rebuilds the site. For each item it shows only the title, source, date, a short excerpt and a link: please read the full text at the original source.

How it updates

Once a day, a GitHub Actions job downloads the feeds, keeps what is on-topic and new, saves it to the repository and publishes the site again. Topics (alignment, interpretability, evaluations, governance, security) are assigned automatically by keywords, so they are a rough guide, not a judgement.

Papers and the Library

Papers and the Library are chosen by hand. Every entry is checked against its original source, and each synopsis is a short summary written for this site, never text copied from the publisher. Book covers and details come from Open Library, the Internet Archive's open catalogue of books; many thanks to them. Links marked "Free version (official)" point only to free editions published by the authors or publishers themselves.

Your To read / Read marks (My shelf) are stored only in your browser; this site has no accounts and collects no data.

Typeface

The text is set in Literata, a typeface for reading on screens by TypeTogether, first made for Google Play Books. It is served from this site itself (no third-party font service), under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
